Purpose and Scope This document specifies the technical requirements for all customers requesting electric service at one of Pacific Gas and Electric Company’s (PG&E) primary distribution voltages as defined in Rule 2. It is intended to give the customer a clear understanding of what their responsibilities are to receive Primary Service (PS) and those of PG&E. PG&E has developed these technical requirements in order to provide safe and reliable service to all the customers the Company serves. This document applies to load and generation PS customers. If the PS customer already has or intends to install distributed generation or back up generation, then also refer to the information described in the PG&E Distribution Interconnection Handbook (DIH). The DIH contains the specific requirements for interconnecting generating facilities or distributed generation (DG) with PG&E electrical distribution system.
